FAQ

Questions, answered.

A few useful details about how PricePeekBD compares products.

Which stores does PricePeekBD compare?

PricePeekBD currently checks the stores listed in the “Currently Compared Stores” section. More stores can be added as integrations become available.

Can I paste a product link?

Yes. Paste a supported product URL into the main search box and PricePeekBD will use the URL comparison flow.

Is the lowest price always the best deal?

Not necessarily. Stock, discount, cashback, coupons, seller status and other offer details can affect the final value, so check the store listing before purchasing.

How fresh are the prices?

Search results are fetched from the supported store integrations and may be cached briefly for performance. Always verify the final price on the store before checkout.

Does PricePeekBD sell the products?

No. PricePeekBD is a comparison interface. The Visit Store button takes you to the marketplace listing.
