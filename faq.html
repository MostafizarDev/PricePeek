Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about searching products, comparing prices, checking deals, and buying directly from the original store.

Search & Comparison

What can I search for on PricePeekBD?

You can search by product name, brand, model, or other product details. You can also paste a supported product link, and image search can help identify a product and turn it into a normal product search.

Can I search by product image?

Yes. Upload a product image or paste an image into the search interface. PricePeekBD can use visible product information and readable text to create a clean search query.

Can I paste a product link?

Yes. Paste a supported store product URL into the main search box. PricePeekBD uses the product information from the listing when available and then compares relevant store results.

How does PricePeekBD find the same product?

PricePeekBD compares information such as product name, brand, model and key specifications to group matching or closely related listings. Variants can still differ, so review the original listing before buying.

Prices & Deals

How current are the prices?

Prices are fetched from supported store integrations and may be cached briefly for performance. Store prices and availability can change, so always verify the final price on the original store before checkout.

Is the lowest price always the best deal?

Not necessarily. Stock status, discounts, coupons, cashback and other offer details can affect the final value. Review the complete store listing before purchasing.

What does Best Price mean?

Best Price identifies the listing with the lowest comparable listed price among the available matching results shown by PricePeekBD.

What does Best Deal mean?

Best Deal is based on the available offer information, such as price and applicable discount or other deal details. The final value can depend on conditions shown by the original store.

Supported Stores

Which stores does PricePeekBD compare?

PricePeekBD compares products across the Bangladesh stores available through its current store integrations. The supported store list can grow as more integrations become available.

Why can't I find a particular store or product?

A store or product may not be available through the current integrations, may block automated access, or may not have a matching listing for the search. Store coverage can change over time.

Will more stores be added?

PricePeekBD is designed around modular store integrations, so additional supported stores can be added over time.

Buying & Price Verification

Does PricePeekBD sell products?

No. PricePeekBD is a price-comparison interface. When you choose Visit Store, you are taken to the original store listing to complete the purchase.

Where do I buy the product?

Buy directly from the original store. PricePeekBD provides the comparison and a link to the store listing; checkout, payment, delivery and seller terms are handled by the store.

Are the prices guaranteed at checkout?

No. Prices, stock, coupons and availability can change. Always confirm the final price and terms on the original store before placing an order.

Privacy & Usage

Do I need an account?

Price comparison is designed to work without requiring a user account.

How does Wishlist work?

Wishlist items are saved in your browser so you can keep products for later comparison. Clearing browser storage can remove locally saved items.
