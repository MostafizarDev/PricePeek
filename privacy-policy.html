Privacy Policy

How PricePeekBD handles information, browser data, image-search inputs, and external-store links.

Last updated: October 1, 2026

1. Overview

PricePeekBD is a product-discovery and price-comparison service. We help users search products and compare publicly available listings from supported Bangladesh stores. We are not a retailer, marketplace, payment processor, delivery service, or seller.

Important: When you leave PricePeekBD and open a store listing, that store's own privacy policy and terms apply.

2. Information We May Receive

  • Search data: product names, brands, models, specifications, and other text you enter.
  • Image-search data: images you upload or paste, plus text extracted from them, may be processed to generate a product search query.
  • Contact data: information you voluntarily send through email, Telegram, WhatsApp, or other contact channels.
  • Technical data: standard request, browser, device, IP, timing, and error information may be processed by hosting or infrastructure providers.
  • Local browser data: wishlist, comparison selections, and preferences may be stored locally on your device.

3. How We Use Information

We may use information to operate searches, image search, wishlist and comparison features; respond to support requests; maintain security; diagnose errors; improve product matching and store integrations; and comply with applicable legal requirements.

The core service does not require your payment-card number, delivery address, or order information.

4. Cookies & Browser Storage

PricePeekBD may use cookies or browser storage for functionality, preferences, security, and user experience. Wishlist and comparison data can be stored locally in your browser without an account. Clearing browser storage can remove those local selections.

5. Image Search

When you use image search, the submitted image and optional extracted text may be sent to the configured image-analysis service to identify useful product information and generate a search query. Do not upload sensitive documents, IDs, payment information, or private photographs unless necessary.

Third-party services have their own privacy policies and terms, which control their processing of information.

6. Store & Product Information

PricePeekBD may display product names, images, prices, availability, discounts, and store URLs obtained from supported store listings. This is product/listing information, not information about your private activity.

Clicking a store link takes you to an independent website. That website may collect information according to its own policies.

7. Sharing & Disclosure

We do not sell personal information as a business practice. Information may be processed by service providers needed for hosting, infrastructure, security, communications, or specific features. We may also disclose information when required by applicable law, legal process, or a valid governmental request.

8. Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures appropriate to the service to protect information. However, no internet service can guarantee absolute security. Avoid sending sensitive information through ordinary contact channels.

9. Retention

Information is retained only as reasonably necessary for service operation, security, support, dispute handling, or legal obligations. Local wishlist and comparison data remains on your device until you clear or remove it.

10. External Websites

PricePeekBD is not responsible for the privacy, security, cookies, content, or data practices of external retailer websites. Review their policies before providing personal or payment information.

11. Your Choices

You can stop using PricePeekBD, clear local browser storage, and choose what information to include in contact messages. For requests concerning information you sent directly to us, contact us through the Contact Us page.

12. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy when our service, technology, providers, or applicable requirements change. The “Last updated” date identifies the current version.

13. Contact

For privacy questions, use the contact channels listed on our Contact Us page.
