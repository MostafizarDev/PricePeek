HOW IT WORKS

Compare before you buy.

PricePeekBD helps you search for a product, compare available prices across supported Bangladesh stores, review the offers, and continue your purchase directly with the original store.

Search

Search by product name, brand, model, or use image search.

Check Price

See available matching listings and compare their prices and offer information.

Visit Store

Open the original product listing on the marketplace or store website.

Buy

Complete your purchase directly on the original store's website.

Step-by-step

Here is what happens from your first search to the final store visit.

01Search a product

Start from the main PricePeekBD search box. Enter a product name, brand, model, or useful product details. You can also use the image-search button to upload or paste a product image.

  • Product name or category
  • Brand and model number
  • Useful specifications such as storage, RAM or size
  • Product image for image-based search

02PricePeek checks supported stores

Your search is sent to the available store integrations. PricePeekBD collects matching product listings and normalizes key information such as title, price, original price, discount, stock status and source URL.

Because store websites can change, results and availability can vary between searches.

03Matching the same product

PricePeekBD uses product information such as brand, model and important specifications to identify listings that appear to represent the same or compatible product. This helps keep different products and variants from being treated as identical.

Always check the original listing for the exact variant, size, storage, warranty and other purchase conditions.

04Compare the results

Results are presented in product cards so you can compare available offers in one place. Depending on the available data, you can use marketplace filters, stock and discount filters, and price or discount sorting.

Best Price and Best Deal labels are based on the available comparison and offer information shown by PricePeekBD.

05Review the store listing

When you find an offer you want to inspect, select Visit Store. PricePeekBD takes you to the original listing so you can review the seller's current information.

  • Confirm the current price
  • Check stock and available variants
  • Review discount, coupon or cashback conditions
  • Check warranty, delivery and seller information where applicable

06Buy directly from the store

PricePeekBD does not sell or fulfill the products shown in comparison results. After you choose a store, your purchase is completed directly through that store's own checkout and policies.

Image Search

When you do not know the exact product name, start with an image.

IMGUpload or paste an image

Use the camera/image button beside the search field, choose a product image, or paste an image with Ctrl + V where supported.

TXTTurn the image into a search query

PricePeekBD can use readable product text and visual product information to create a cleaner product query. The detected query is placed into the search box so you can edit it before searching.

Important: verify before you buy

PricePeekBD is a comparison tool, not the seller. Store prices, stock, discounts, coupons, cashback and product details can change. Always confirm the final price, exact product/variant and purchase terms on the original store before placing an order.
